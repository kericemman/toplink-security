Bring one live issue.

The session focuses on one situation that needs your attention now. It may involve:

  • A consequential security decision
  • Risk acceptance or a security exception
  • A supplier or third-party proposal
  • An executive or board conversation
  • An escalation or stakeholder disagreement
  • Competing security and business priorities
  • Unclear ownership or decision authority
  • A difficult leadership situation with security implications

You do not need to commission an assessment, prepare a presentation or assemble formal documentation. Bring the situation as you understand it.

What we examine.

We examine the decision under consideration, the information available, the assumptions being made and the security and business implications.

We test your professional position, identify questions that still need answers and consider how to communicate the issue to the people who hold the relevant authority.

The purpose is not to tell you what to think. It is to help you reach a considered position and determine a practical next move.

After the session: Decision Note™

You receive a concise written record covering:

  • The situation examined
  • The decision under consideration
  • The principal issues examined
  • Your professional position
  • The next move

The note supports your follow-through. It is not a formal risk assessment or an organizational decision record.
